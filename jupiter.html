One place for the whole business.

How the AI works

Nova drafts. I decide.

AI does the legwork underneath: it reads, drafts, and suggests. Anything that touches a client, money, or a record waits for a person to approve it, and every action is logged.

  1. DraftNova writes the next move. A report, a reply, a task.
  2. ReviewI approve it, edit it, or throw it out.
  3. CheckThe system checks it again. Permissions and details, before anything runs.
  4. LogEvery action is recorded. Who approved it, what changed, and when.
