THE OPERATING CHALLENGE

A sale does not happen on one screen.

The customer has to imagine the result. The business has to capture the right details. The team has to price, document, schedule, deliver, and follow up.

That chain is where information gets repeated, delayed, or lost. The Southern Pavers system was designed to keep the project context moving—from the first digital interaction through the work itself.

JPAX PrincipleDesign the whole journey, not a pile of disconnected tools.

THE CONNECTED JOURNEY

One project record. Seven critical moments.

Each step carries useful context forward instead of asking the next person—or the customer—to rebuild it.

  1. 01

    Attract

    The public site creates demand and records the source, page, and action that started the journey.

  2. 02

    Visualize

    The homeowner uses a real property photo to explore an AI-assisted project direction.

  3. 03

    Qualify

    Project type, approximate size, preferences, contact details, and concept images become one structured request.

  4. 04

    Estimate

    The private workspace combines project context with the live pricing catalog to draft a reviewable estimate.

  5. 05

    Approve

    Contracts, invoices, add-ons, and secure signature links stay connected to the customer record.

  6. 06

    Deliver

    Scheduling, build notes, files, and work photos give the team one operational view of the project.

  7. 07

    Learn

    Attribution, funnel signals, completed work, and review follow-up close the loop.
