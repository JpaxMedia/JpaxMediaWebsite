About

I'm Julian. I do the work myself.

I'm local, here in Greenville. I spent years making businesses look good before I started fixing how they run.

I kept seeing the same thing. Good shops doing good work, losing money in the gaps: the call nobody answered, the estimate nobody chased, the review nobody asked for. JPAX is what I built to close those gaps. I find the leak, install the system that stops it, and keep it running.

FROM JULIAN“I don't sell pieces. The system works because everything works together.”

How I got here

I didn't start in systems.

I started making things look good in 2018. Then I kept getting pulled one layer deeper, until I was working on the part that actually decides whether a shop grows.

  1. DesignFreelance design for small businesses. Logos, print, the look of the thing. I learned fast that a good look doesn't answer the phone.
  2. CameraPhotography and video. Shooting for businesses put me inside them, close enough to see where the day falls apart.
  3. ScreenWebsites, then apps. I built what came after the click: the forms, the follow-up, the tools behind the page.
  4. June 2025JPAX, here in Greenville. I stopped selling pieces and started fixing the whole handoff: call, estimate, job, review.
  5. TodayAudit, Install, Partner. For owners who already have the work and are tired of watching it slip.

How I work

A few rules I don't bend.

  • I don't work for your competitors.If you lay pavers in Greenville, no other paver company in your area gets a seat. Your audit holds it for 30 days. The install locks it.
  • I don't sell pieces.The audit decides what gets built, and it all works together.
  • You own what we install.Your numbers, your accounts, your website, your data.
