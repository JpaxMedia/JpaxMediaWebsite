Privacy

Privacy policy.

What this site collects, why, and what you can ask me to do about it. Effective September 27, 2026.

This site is run by JPAX Media LLC in Greenville, South Carolina ("JPAX," "I," "me"). If you have a question about anything here, email julian@jpaxmedia.com.

What you send me

When you fill out the booking form, I get what you type: your name, business name, type of business, average job value, the area you serve, phone number, email, what you're asking about, and any notes. I use it to reply to you, schedule a call, and scope work you ask about. That's it.

What the site measures

The site uses the JPAX Pixel, my own first-party analytics. It records which pages are viewed, clicks on buttons, phone and email links, how far down a page you scroll, time on the page, the referring site and campaign tags, device type, browser language and time zone, and an approximate location (city, region, country). It stores random visitor and session IDs in your browser so repeat visits can be counted.

It does not record your raw IP address. The IP is turned into a one-way hash before it's stored. It does not identify you by name unless you give me your name yourself, for example through the form or a link I sent you directly.

Who else handles it

  • Netlify hosts this site and receives form submissions.
  • Supabase stores the analytics data.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.

I don't sell your information, and I don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

How long I keep it

I keep form submissions as long as they're useful for working with you or answering your request, and analytics data as long as it's useful for understanding how the site performs. You can ask me to delete either at any time.

Your choices

  • Email julian@jpaxmedia.com to see, correct, or delete what you've sent me.
  • Clearing your browser's site data resets the visitor ID. Blocking scripts stops the analytics entirely, and the site still works.

Children

This site is for business owners. It isn't meant for anyone under 16, and I don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top.
